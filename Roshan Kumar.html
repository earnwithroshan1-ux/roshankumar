About

Sales is a skill, and you can practise it.

I'm Roshan Kumar, a sales expert. I started this group because most people in their early twenties are never taught how to sell, yet every job, side hustle and business depends on it.

Selling is helping someone make a decision. In the group I explain what works in plain language, with examples you can try the same day, so you build confidence by doing and not by reading theory.

Is this group for you?

  • You are between 19 and 21 years old.
  • You want to earn through sales, freelancing or a side hustle.
  • You get nervous talking to people you don't know.
  • You want clear steps you can try today, not long lectures.

How I teach

Practise out loud

Reading about sales helps a little. Saying the words out loud helps a lot.

Small asks first

Start with a low-pressure question, then build up to asking for the decision.

Listen more than you pitch

The best sellers ask good questions and let the other person talk.
